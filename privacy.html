2. Scope

This policy explains which personal data we process when you visit rotvideo.ch. The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) applies. Where visitors from the EU use our website, we also observe the principles of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc. When the page is requested, Vercel automatically processes technical data sent by the browser, namely the IP address, date and time of the request, the page requested, browser type and operating system. These log files serve the technical delivery and security of the website and are not combined with other data sources.

4. Reach measurement (Vercel Analytics & Speed Insights)

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to evaluate visitor numbers and loading times. Both services work without cookies and do not create personal usage profiles. They record aggregated data such as pages viewed, approximate geographic region (at country or city level, derived from the IP address) and device type. We cannot attribute this data to individual persons.

5. Google Ads (conversion tracking)

We use the Google tag (gtag.js) from Google Ireland Ltd. to measure which actions on our website are connected to clicks on our Google ads (conversion tracking). This tag is only loaded once you actively consent in the cookie banner. If you consent, Google sets cookies that store a unique identifier showing that someone reached our website from one of our ads. As a rule, we cannot attribute the information collected to you personally. You can withdraw or renew your consent at any time via the “Cookie settings” link in the footer. Further information is available in Google's privacy policy and under Google Ads cookies.

6. Google Fonts

We use fonts from Google Fonts, which are loaded directly from Google's servers when the page is opened. In the process, the IP address of the requesting device is transmitted to Google. More in Google's privacy policy.

7. YouTube videos

On our website we show reference films as embedded YouTube videos. The preview images of these videos are loaded directly from YouTube's servers (Google Ireland Ltd.) when the page is opened. In the process, the IP address of the requesting device is transmitted to Google. The video player itself is only loaded when a video is actively clicked. With that click, Google receives, among other things, the IP address and information about usage behaviour. Details are available in Google's privacy policy.

8. Contacting us

For enquiries we link directly to email (info@rotvideo.ch) and phone (+41 79 892 82 86). There is no contact form on this website that transmits data to our server. We process the information you give us by email or phone solely to answer your enquiry and delete it as soon as it is no longer needed for that purpose.

9. Social media links

In the footer we link to our profiles on Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn. These are simple links, not embedded social plugins. When you click them you leave our website, and the privacy terms of the respective provider apply.

10. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law you have the right to information about the personal data processed, and to rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and release of your data. To exercise these rights, contact info@rotvideo.ch.

11. Changes

We update this privacy policy whenever our website or the legal requirements change. The version published at the time of your visit applies.
